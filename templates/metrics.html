Data coverage & definitions

Total MIPs and current-state cards count existing cases once, including imports/restores and missing timestamps. Deleted cases are excluded. Board filters are independent of this unfiltered inventory. No completion dates are inferred from current state.

For activity periods, completion is the final recorded Packaging & Delivery stage exit leading to Completed, or preceding a recorded reopening. Each case counts once per reporting period, using its first qualifying completion in that period; recompletion in a later period can count again. Completed does not mean delivered.

Cycle time uses creation to that completion event ({{performance.timed_cases}} valid records). {{performance.timing_excluded}} selected-period completions have unavailable cycle time. {{performance.missing_completion}} currently completed cases have no usable timestamp for their current completion; no dates are guessed. {{performance.invalid_creation}} cases have missing, invalid, or future creation dates. Legacy naive timestamps are read as UTC. Imported history may predate creation; negative durations are excluded.
